# PDF export

Export a PDF copy of your GitBook content

### Allow readers to export a PDF version of your published content

To enable or disable PDF export for visitors to your [published docs site](/docs/publish/publish-a-docs-site), open **Customize**, under **Tools** in the site sidebar, and navigate to **Configure → Page actions**. From there, you can toggle **Export as PDF** on or off.

This setting determines whether or not **readers of your published content can download it in PDF format**. This feature is only available for **Premium and Ultimate sites**.

### Export your own internal content as PDF

However you decide to configure your published docs sites, all logged-in members of an organization on a Pro or Enterprise can export a page — or an entire section — from your internal knowledge base as a PDF file.

Note that links across sections are not currently supported when exporting internal content to PDF.

#### Export an individual page

1. Open the page you want to export, then open the page’s [Actions menu](/docs/reference/gitbook-ui#the-actions-menu)

   ![](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252F89MTSo5XRpPMVr1T0rxS%252Factions.svg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D2b5d001e-560a-4f29-8d22-de8163725ca1&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=45b786a9&sv=2)![](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252FQ4IsWwmEEi5QM7PSXNsN%252Factions%2520-%2520dark.svg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Debff54f4-9825-4ab0-99bc-633e1c449371&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=7ea56dee&sv=2)

   The Actions menu icon in GitBook

   next to the page title.
2. Select **Export to PDF > Current page**.
3. Wait for the page to load, then click the **Print or save as PDF** button in the upper right to open your browsers Print menu.
4. From here, you can save the page as a PDF or open it in your PDF viewer using the typical process for your browser.

#### Export an entire section

1. Open the [Actions menu](/docs/create-content/content-structure)

   ![](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252F89MTSo5XRpPMVr1T0rxS%252Factions.svg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D2b5d001e-560a-4f29-8d22-de8163725ca1&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=45b786a9&sv=2)![](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252FQ4IsWwmEEi5QM7PSXNsN%252Factions%2520-%2520dark.svg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Debff54f4-9825-4ab0-99bc-633e1c449371&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=7ea56dee&sv=2)

   The Actions menu icon in GitBook

   next to the page title and choose **Export as PDF > All pages**. Alternatively, open the section’s **Actions menu**

   ![](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252FPnnI41SqLSaKBNwT98fW%252Factions-horizontal.svg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D99754200-a354-4ffe-931e-aa6322ea7395&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=81456b6f&sv=2)![](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252Fpn5vEw7bYFrMPpdyvpSu%252Factions-horizontal%2520-%2520dark.svg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Dec39eefe-a391-4fe2-828a-082b79f2847d&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=c1d57f4a&sv=2)

   The Actions menu icon in GitBook

   in the [section header](/docs/reference/gitbook-ui#space-header) and choose **Export as PDF** in the drop-down menu.
   &#xNAN;*Note: This action is not available within a change request.*
2. Wait for the page to load, then click the **Print or save as PDF** button in the upper right to open your browsers Print menu.
3. From here, you can save the page as a PDF or open it in your PDF viewer using the typical process for your browser.
