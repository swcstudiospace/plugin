## Documentation your users actually read

Your users are humans and their AI agents — GitBook publishes for both. Write in the editor, drive it from your agent, or sync from Git.

[Quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart)[GitBook MCP](/docs/docs-as-code/gitbook-mcp)

### How do you want to work?

The same docs, three ways in. Most teams use more than one.

####

#### In the editor

Write and publish visually. Blocks, change requests, and review without touching a terminal.

[Quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart)

[Format content](/docs/create-content/formatting)

[Publish a docs site](/docs/publish/publish-a-docs-site)

#### From your agent

Drive GitBook from Claude, Cursor, or any MCP client. Draft, restructure, and open change requests.

### AI out of the box

Two work for you while you write. Three work for your readers after you publish.

WRITES WITH YOU

#### GitBook Agent

Drafts, edits, reviews change requests, and flags gaps against your style guide.

FINDS THINGS

#### AI Search

Natural-language search across your content, in the app and on your published site.

ANSWERS READERS

#### GitBook Assistant

Answers questions on your site from your docs and connected support sources.

IMPROVES DOCS

#### AI Insights

See what your visitors are asking and identify knowledge gaps in your docs.

SERVES AGENTS

#### MCP for your docs

Your published docs as a tool, so your customers' agents can read them properly.
