# Cards

Display information more dynamically with a set of cards — with or without images

You can use cards to create a visually pleasing page layout, combining text and images in a grid. They’re ideal for building landing pages or displaying any other content in a non-linear way.

You can adjust [switch between medium or large cards](/docs/create-content/blocks/cards#changing-the-size-of-cards) and link them to the relevant resources.

### Adding links

Hover over a card and open its **Options menu** ![The Options menu icon in GitBook](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252FaS1QvPIBVYwhpFTGcPBN%252Foptions-menu.svg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D3ee40bbf-f4fb-41fa-aa30-306b559cbe88&width=300&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=593d591b&sv=2)![The Options menu icon in GitBook](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252FjYRg42UtM4u1pHmJl4Ln%252Fdrag%2520-%2520dark.svg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D4c219b2b-37d2-449e-9130-19b6ba3d38d2&width=300&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=dac31aad&sv=2). Here you can add a target link, so readers can jump directly to a location when they click the card.

When creating cards, we recommend you use **target links instead of hyperlinks**. With a target link, your readers can click anywhere on the card to access the linked URL.

### Adding images

Hover over a card and open its **Options menu** ![The Options menu icon in GitBook](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252FaS1QvPIBVYwhpFTGcPBN%252Foptions-menu.svg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D3ee40bbf-f4fb-41fa-aa30-306b559cbe88&width=300&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=593d591b&sv=2)![The Options menu icon in GitBook](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252FjYRg42UtM4u1pHmJl4Ln%252Fdrag%2520-%2520dark.svg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D4c219b2b-37d2-449e-9130-19b6ba3d38d2&width=300&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=dac31aad&sv=2). Here you can add a cover image to your card. Alternatively, just click the **Add cover image** option on the card itself.

This will open the **Select file** modal. Here you can drag and drop a new image into this, or use an image file you’ve previously uploaded to your section.

#### Adding images for dark mode

You can also add cover images that will only show in dark mode.

To do this, open the card’s **Options menu** ![](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252FaS1QvPIBVYwhpFTGcPBN%252Foptions-menu.svg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D3ee40bbf-f4fb-41fa-aa30-306b559cbe88&width=300&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=593d591b&sv=2)![](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252FjYRg42UtM4u1pHmJl4Ln%252Fdrag%2520-%2520dark.svg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D4c219b2b-37d2-449e-9130-19b6ba3d38d2&width=300&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=dac31aad&sv=2) and choose **Cover** > **Edit cover** > **Add cover for dark mode**. This will open the **Select file** modal, where you can drag and drop a new image or select a previously-uploaded image.

#### Choosing the right image size

GitBook will automatically crop landscape images to a 16:9 ratio on desktop and mobile. If the images you upload are portrait or have a 1:1 ratio, they will be cropped to 16:9 on desktop and display as square or portrait on mobile.

![A GitBook screenshot showing card images on desktop](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252FnX5tZdJgW1Yhf61ZXpGJ%252F26_01_06_cards_desktop%25402x.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Da49b6556-194d-47ce-a13b-773561703f5b&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=aeb36a03&sv=2)

On desktop, all card images will display in a landscape 16:9 ratio, regardless of their dimensions. We recommend using the same dimensions for consistency.

![A GitBook screenshot showing card images on mobile](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252FxUUSyA6H7QwA8p9Nihz1%252F26_01_06_cards_mobile%25402x.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Ddf76e62d-dfb4-4197-a140-48d268c52240&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=14bed823&sv=2)

On mobile, square or portrait images display as shown on the left. Landscape images display as shown on the right.

To keep things consistent across desktop and mobile, we recommend uploading all the images for your cards in a 16:9 format (e.g. 1920px x 1080px).

If you want your cards to adapt their layout depending on the screen size, we’d recommend uploading images with a 1:1 ratio, and the content of your image centered.

### Changing the size of cards

You can select the card size by opening the **Options menu** ![The Options menu icon in GitBook](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252FaS1QvPIBVYwhpFTGcPBN%252Foptions-menu.svg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D3ee40bbf-f4fb-41fa-aa30-306b559cbe88&width=300&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=593d591b&sv=2)![The Options menu icon in GitBook](https://gitbook.com/docs/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1050631731-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X%252Fuploads%252FjYRg42UtM4u1pHmJl4Ln%252Fdrag%2520-%2520dark.svg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D4c219b2b-37d2-449e-9130-19b6ba3d38d2&width=300&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=dac31aad&sv=2) to the left of your card block. The **Medium** option creates three cards in one horizontal line, while the **Large** option shows two larger cards on each line.
